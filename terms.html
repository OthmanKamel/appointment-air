1. Service description

Appointment Air helps appointment-based businesses, providers, and clinics send and manage appointment- related communications, including reminders, confirmations, rescheduling instructions, reschedule confirmations, and earlier appointment availability notifications.

Appointment Air is a software and communication automation service. It is not a medical provider, emergency service, legal service, financial service, or substitute for direct communication with the business providing the appointment.

2. SMS messaging program

Program name: Appointment Air

Program description: Appointment reminder and scheduling text messages for appointment-based businesses, providers, and clinics.

Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on appointment activity.

Message and data rates may apply.

For help, reply HELP. To opt out, reply STOP.

Customers may opt in to SMS appointment reminders through an Appointment Air opt-in page provided by the business, provider, or clinic where the appointment is booked. These messages may include appointment reminders, confirmation requests, rescheduling instructions, reschedule confirmations, and earlier appointment availability notifications.

For security, the business may send a final confirmation email soon before SMS reminders are enabled.

3. Opt-out and help instructions

Recipients can reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages. After opting out, the recipient should no longer receive SMS messages from that Appointment Air messaging program unless they opt in again or otherwise provide new valid consent.

Recipients can reply HELP for help. For appointment-specific questions, recipients should contact the business where the appointment was booked.

4. SMS opt-in

Appointment Air provides an SMS opt-in workflow that businesses, providers, and clinics can direct customers to before appointment reminder text messages are enabled. The opt-in workflow asks for the business name, customer contact details, appointment information, email for final confirmation, and optional SMS consent.

SMS consent is optional and unchecked by default. Customers should not receive Appointment Air SMS messages unless they have opted in or otherwise provided valid consent. Customers can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

5. Business-side use

Businesses, providers, and clinics are responsible for using Appointment Air only for appointment-related communication, entering accurate appointment information, and respecting customer SMS consent choices.

Businesses should not enter sensitive or unnecessary information into Appointment Air that is not needed for appointment reminder or scheduling purposes.
