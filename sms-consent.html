Opt in for appointment follow-ups

If you have requested help booking or rescheduling an appointment through Appointment Air's conversational AI assistance, this page explains how to opt in to optional SMS follow-ups about your request.

SMS opt-in is optional. You can still request voice assistance or contact the business directly for appointment guidance. If you receive SMS follow-ups, reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

What follow-ups are for: responses and updates about customer-initiated requests to set or reschedule an appointment, including available-time options and booking confirmations. This is not a marketing program. Message frequency varies based on appointment activity. Message and data rates may apply.

SMS opt-in form

This page only creates an opt-in record in your browser. It does not send your information to the business, confirm an appointment, or activate SMS reminders. Contact the business directly for actual follow-up.
