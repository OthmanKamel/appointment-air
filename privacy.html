Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Appointment Air by Othman Kamel (“Appointment Air,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with appointment reminder and scheduling communications.

Appointment Air is an information technology service used by appointment-based businesses, providers, and clinics to send appointment reminders, confirmations, rescheduling instructions, and earlier appointment availability notifications to customers who opt in to receive those messages.

SMS privacy commitment: We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

1. Information we may process

Appointment Air may process limited information needed to send and manage appointment-related messages, including:

  • Customer or patient name, when provided;
  • Business, provider, or clinic name;
  • Mobile phone number and email address;
  • Appointment date and time;
  • SMS consent status and opt-in information;
  • Final email confirmation status, when used;
  • Appointment confirmation, rescheduling, and response status;
  • Message delivery information, such as timestamps and response keywords;
  • Appointment-related notes needed by the business using Appointment Air.

Businesses using Appointment Air should avoid entering sensitive information that is not needed for appointment reminders or scheduling.

2. How information is used

We use information to operate Appointment Air and provide appointment-related communication features, including:

  • Recording SMS opt-in choices;
  • Supporting final confirmation by email when used;
  • Sending appointment reminders;
  • Receiving appointment confirmations;
  • Supporting rescheduling requests;
  • Notifying customers about earlier appointment availability;
  • Helping businesses review appointment communication status and follow-up needs;
  • Maintaining logs needed for service reliability, troubleshooting, and compliance.

3. SMS consent and opt-out

Customers may opt in to appointment reminder and scheduling text messages through an Appointment Air opt-in page provided by the business, provider, or clinic where the appointment is booked. The opt-in page asks for the business name, customer contact details, appointment information, email for final confirmation, and optional SMS consent.

SMS consent is optional and must be affirmative. Customers can choose not to receive SMS appointment reminders. For security, the business may send a final confirmation email before SMS reminders are enabled. Message frequency varies based on appointment activity. Message and data rates may apply. Recipients can reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages. Recipients can reply HELP for help.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share or process information only as needed to operate the service, such as with communication, hosting, database, messaging, voice, email, or infrastructure providers that help deliver Appointment Air. These providers are used for operational purposes, not for selling or marketing mobile consent information.

5. Data retention

Appointment-related records may be kept for as long as needed to operate the service, support the business using Appointment Air, maintain logs, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or improve service reliability.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information handled by Appointment Air. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we aim to limit access to information based on operational need.

7. Business responsibility

Appointment Air is a software service. Businesses, providers, and clinics using Appointment Air are responsible for directing customers to the correct opt-in workflow, entering accurate appointment information, and responding to appointment questions that require human follow-up.

Businesses should not use Appointment Air to send SMS messages to customers who have not opted in or otherwise provided valid SMS consent.

8. Children

Appointment Air is intended for use by businesses and is not directed to children. Businesses should not use Appointment Air to knowingly collect information from children without appropriate authorization and consent.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about Appointment Air, contact: appointmentair@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
