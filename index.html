How Appointment Air works

Appointment Air offers human-like AI voice assistance and empathetic, conversational responses to help customers with booking and rescheduling. It provides clear appointment guidance, not unsolicited sales outreach or marketing campaigns.

1. Customer reaches out

A customer reaches out to request a new appointment or change an existing booking.

2. Appointment Air assists

The AI assistant responds with a human-like voice and helpful guidance, checks available times, and assists with appointment setting and rescheduling.

3. Details and consent are handled

Customers receive clear guidance about their appointment details. Any optional follow-up remains subject to their communication preferences and applicable consent requirements.

Business subscriptions

Appointment Air provides subscription-based appointment assistance software to businesses. Plans and any prepaid service periods are offered under a written quote or order that explains the included features, service start date, charges, billing currency, renewal terms, and any promotional offer before payment. Pricing is quoted in U.S. dollars (USD) unless stated otherwise.

Just one additional successfully booked appointment per month may cover the subscription cost, depending on the value of the appointment. We never charge a setup fee. Three- and six-month prepaid options are available with additional services and customization, detailed in the quote.

Service access is provided digitally; no physical products are shipped. For subscription options, onboarding, billing questions, cancellations, or refund requests, contact admin.appointmentair@gmail.com. See our subscription and billing terms and cancellation and refund information.

SMS opt-in

For customer-initiated booking or rescheduling requests, a business can direct a customer to the Appointment Air opt-in page to review optional SMS appointment follow-ups.

The opt-in page asks for the business name, contact details, and appointment information. The SMS checkbox is optional and unchecked by default. The page currently creates a local opt-in record only; it does not transmit details, register a customer, or book an appointment.

For an active SMS conversation, customers can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies with requested appointment follow-up. Message and data rates may apply.
