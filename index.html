SMS opt-in, handled clearly.

For customer-initiated booking or rescheduling requests, a business can direct customers to the separate SMS opt-in page to review optional appointment follow-ups. This is not an unsolicited marketing program.

The form has an optional, unchecked SMS opt-in checkbox. It currently prepares a record locally in the customer's browser only; it does not transmit details, register the customer, book an appointment, or activate follow-ups.

For an active SMS conversation, recipients can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply.
