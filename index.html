How Appointment Air works

Appointment Air supports the operational side of appointment communication. It does not replace the business relationship between the business and its customer.

1. Business collects consent

The business collects the customer’s phone number and confirms permission to send appointment-related SMS messages before entering or importing the number.

2. Appointment Air sends reminders

Appointment Air can send reminders, confirmation prompts, rescheduling instructions, and earlier-slot notifications.

3. Customer controls SMS

Recipients can reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages or HELP for assistance.

SMS consent model

Customers provide their phone number when booking or managing an appointment with a business that uses Appointment Air. The business informs the customer that they may receive appointment reminder and scheduling text messages sent using Appointment Air.

Businesses using Appointment Air should confirm SMS consent before sending messages. Recipients can reply STOP to opt out at any time. Message frequency varies based on appointments. Message and data rates may apply.
